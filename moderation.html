Moderation in intercultural contexts

I moderate discussions, panels and working sessions where more than one language, or more than one working culture, is in the room. I work in German and English, from Berlin, and travel where the job needs it.

Panels · Workshops · Facilitation · Speaker preparation
Approach

Discussions go wrong in predictable ways. The most fluent speaker takes over, people in their second language go quiet, and real disagreement gets smoothed into a summary nobody actually meant. A moderator's job is to see that coming and stop it before it starts.

Most of my work is panels, public discussions and internal working sessions in German and English. I prepare thoroughly, agree the structure with you in advance, and keep to it on the day. The standard is simple: everyone in the room gets heard, the meeting reaches its aim, and nobody's time is wasted.

What I do

Services

Bilingual panels and public discussions

Panel discussions and public conversations that run in both German and English, moving between the two as needed.

90–120 minutes, on stage or in the round; preparation call included.

Workshops across cultures

Internal workshops where teams from different countries or working cultures need to leave with a decision, not just a good conversation.

Half-day or full-day; groups up to 30.

Facilitation of difficult meetings

Structure for meetings where there is real tension in the room: restructurings, strained partnerships, communities that disagree.

Planned case by case after a confidential call.

Speaker and programme preparation

Help for speakers, moderators and organisers getting ready for an event: questions, timings, transitions, and speaking in a second language.

Typically two to three sessions before the event.
Process

Working together

01 Briefing

A 30-minute call about the room, the aim and what could go wrong. This call costs nothing.

02 Preparation

I draft the structure, the questions and the language plan, and agree them with you before the day.

03 On the day

I moderate the event and send you a short written debrief within a week.

Language plan

Languages

I work in German and English and switch between the two mid-discussion when the room needs it. That covers public stage conversations as well as internal sessions and formal institutional settings. The language plan for your event is agreed in the briefing call.
